## Day one — Osaka to Onomichi

Take the early Sanyo Shinkansen out of Shin-Osaka and you are eating ramen in Onomichi before noon. The transfer at Fukuyama is across the platform, which matters when the connection is eight minutes and you are carrying a week of luggage.

From the station it is ten minutes on foot to the ropeway, and the hill behind town is where the day actually starts. Most people do this as a day trip from Hiroshima; staying the night is the better call, because the town empties out after five.

## What the pass will not do

It stops at Hakata. The Tokaido Shinkansen towards Tokyo and the Kyushu Shinkansen south of Hakata are both outside it, so this is a westward pass and nothing else. The Miyajima ferry is included, but the smaller island routes across the inland sea are run by other operators — budget <Price jpy={5600} /> in cash for those boats and the local buses over the week.
