Hiroshima is the first place most people go west of Osaka, and almost everyone arrives at Kansai Airport without having worked out how they are getting there. The honest answer is that there is no clever route. There are three, they are not close in quality, and which one is right depends on a single thing: **whether you are stopping anywhere else in the west.**

## There is no direct route

Kansai Airport is on an island south of Osaka, and Hiroshima is 300 kilometres west of it. Nothing runs between them directly. **Every option goes through Shin-Osaka**, and the only real question is what you do once you are there.

That also answers the question people ask most often. Chugoku JR Bus runs a Hiroshima–Osaka highway bus from <Price jpy={3500} />, but **no service starts at the airport**. A bus journey means getting yourself into Osaka first, which costs you the airport leg anyway and then four hours or so on top.

The HARUKA One-way Ticket is a discounted fare JR-WEST sells to **holders of a foreign passport entering as a Temporary Visitor** — one ticket per person, passport shown at purchase. Shin-Osaka is <Price jpy={1800} />, Kyoto <Price jpy={2200} />, Tennoji <Price jpy={1300} />, Kobe <Price jpy={2000} />, Nara <Price jpy={1800} />. Children are half.

It is an ordinary-car reserved seat, and the reservation is made separately at a machine or a ticket office. Worth doing with luggage.

## The number that decides it

The pass is <Price jpy={22000} /> for seven consecutive days and covers the Sanyo Shinkansen between Shin-Osaka and Hakata, JR-WEST and **JR-SHIKOKU** conventional lines, and the Miyajima ferry. The Tokaido Shinkansen towards Tokyo and the Kyushu Shinkansen south of Hakata are outside it.

So the arithmetic is not "airport to Hiroshima". It is **everything you will do in those seven days**. A return trip to Hiroshima and nothing else will not reach <Price jpy={22000} />. Add Onomichi, add Miyajima, add the crossing to Matsuyama — and the pass stops being a question.

That last one is the part people miss. **JR-SHIKOKU is inside this pass.** If Shikoku is anywhere in your plans, the comparison is already over.

## What I would do

Land, take the Haruka to Shin-Osaka, and do not go west the same day. The flight has already been long, Shin-Osaka is a station rather than a place, and the first morning of a trip is worth more than the last evening of a flight.

Then start west with the pass the following morning, if you are going further than Hiroshima.
